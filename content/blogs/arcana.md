### Venue  

The venue was super cool and happening. We had enough space and had a fun time there. However, we didn't have access to the venue for ~6 hours on the second day of the hackathon (i.e. 1st Aug). That was kind of a bummer as we were forced to leave and explore the city. I did explore the city and had SO MUCH FUN. But since we *had* to vacate in the early morning, I (and about 60% of others) who slept late at night lost the flexibility to have more sleep or work on projects. *(P.S. the orgs did communicate this limitation to us and insisted we sleep — but hey, it's a hackathon after all 🫠.)*  

### Food   

I appreciate the fact that there were enough vegetarian and vegan food options. The amount of food and snacks was ample. There might be some edge cases that I am not aware of, but judging based on how nice and helpful everyone from the organizing team was, I am pretty sure they handled it well. I have no complaints with the food arrangements whatsoever.  

### The sleeping situation  

So before the event, we were told (in the #senators Slack channel) that they didn't have enough mattresses for everyone, so we'd have to arrange sleeping bags ourselves. I didn't pay much attention to this and didn't bring a mattress. Things started to get more complicated when a **lot** of people didn't bring a sleeping bag and ended up sleeping on tables, floors, or chairs.

I was told that there would be mattresses for everyone the next night, but that also didn't happen. Though I understand that I am in no position to blame them for this, as they communicated it to us, I still wanna ask: what caused a lack of mattresses? Like if it was a budget situation, why not add it to the shop?  

### The people!  

First of all, everybody from staff and the organizing team was super nice and helpful. Though I personally didn't spend much time with them, as they had lots of work to do, they helped me multiple times, and at any point during the event and conversations over Slack, I didn't feel like I couldn't say/ask something to them. I interacted with almost everyone from the team. I know a person who said that a particular organizer was "mean" to them, but they gave me no proof whatsoever, and based on my experience with the organizers, I don't think that is true. **If somebody experienced this, you must report it via Shroud.**

As for the participants, I had a nice experience with most of the people. There were some not-so-nice and/or annoying people (based on interactions over Slack as well as the IRL event). I have found people use slur words and disrespect others while taking advantage of the language barrier. For example, there was a case where a person said horrible stuff about an organizer right in front of them, and the organizer didn't understand it because they didn't speak Hindi. I feel deeply saddened that this happened 😔. The org was trying to help that person, and that person was just being super rude to them. Apart from that, there was an insane (imo) amount of slur words used "casually." I've attended other flagship events before and this is the first time I felt this. There were a few other cases as well, but it's not appropriate to discuss them here, and they've already been communicated to the orgs.  

I made about 4 new friends and they were really nice and fun people. **YAYYYY!!!!!**

### Travel and accommodation logistics 🚄  

The organizers worked really really hard to apply for the visas of the participants and made the whole process very convenient. The process was near perfect. Though it wasn't *needed*, it would've been nicer if the visas were applied for earlier, because from what I know, parents of a lot of participants (especially the ones who were traveling to an HC event for the first time) were really worried.

Huge shoutout to **Daamin**, **Karthik**, and **Manan** for working so hard to get our visas.

I (and about 8 other people) was picked up from the airport by **Anson** and I was given an MRT card. This was seamless and I didn't face any issues. I also stayed in post-accommodation and that was also quite seamless, and **Aly** helped us with checking in and other needs.
